# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis loaded from a CDN, no build step, so students can edit copy directly. Deploy target: Vercel or GitHub Pages (Vercel connector available).

## Users

All audiences weighted equally (user choice):
- **Prospective members** (CAJCS Grades 8–11): deciding whether to join a brand-new team.
- **Parents and school**: need trust and safety signals.
- **Sponsors** (companies, alumni): deciding whether to fund a rookie team's first season and the trip to Istanbul.
- **Judges and the *FIRST* community**: looking for process, outreach and Gracious Professionalism.
- **Other teams**: want to collaborate or scout.

## Product Purpose

The public home of The Ducktators, FRC 11745. It introduces a team formed in 2026, explains what FRC is to people who have never heard of it, shows the season plan (Kickoff, build, Istanbul Regional), and turns visitors into members, sponsors and allies. Success = sponsor enquiries, recruit sign-ups, and a site judges can point at.

## Positioning

"A regiment of builders." Duck + dictators pun played as regimental discipline with a wink: officer cap, monocle, collar stars, ranks, orders. The only FRC team from CAJCS Mumbai, a rookie heading to the Istanbul Regional in its first season. Hard boundary: the joke is the duck and the regiment, never real politics, regimes, flags, salutes or insignia.

## Operating Context

- Home base: CAJCS Maker Lab (blue wall, white lettering, ceiling light).
- Team runs its own internal web app (tasks, attendance, 154-task skills library). That is internal; the website is public.
- Season 2026–27: game revealed at Kickoff (early Jan 2027), Istanbul Regional (dates TBA, historically early March).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed facts: name, number 11745, school CAJCS, Mumbai, season 2026–27, registered for Istanbul Regional, mentors Sujal Patil (Programming) and Suyog Mestry (Mechanical, Drive coach), 7 subteams, positions list, ~10 students.
- Unknown and must stay placeholders: game name, event dates, sponsors, robot name, results/awards, social handles, email, student names and photos.

User asked for the palette to be enhanced, and for the bible to be a reference, not a template.

- Voice: confident, witty, disciplined, warm. Primary tagline "Order in the ranks." (pending mentor approval).

## Evidence on Hand

- Badge PNG (1024²) and wordmark JPG; app icons at 64/192/512.
- No team photo, robot photos, sponsor logos, results, social handles or email yet. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Honest rookie: show the plan and the people-shaped gaps instead of faking a history.
2. Explain FRC to outsiders in one scroll.
3. Every audience gets a clear next action (enlist, sponsor, contact, collaborate).
4. Students stay real and protected: no names or faces without consent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visitors include students and parents (often on phones). WCAG AA text contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, alt text on images, works without JS for content.
