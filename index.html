Our mission

Our mission is to give students at Cathedral a real engineering team: design it, build it, compete with it, and share everything we learn so more young people in Mumbai can do the same.

What we stand for.

  1. Discipline by design

    Order in the ranks means process: plan the work, document every decision, test before we trust, and finish what we start.

  2. Students lead

    Mentors teach and keep us safe. Students make the calls, hold the tools and own the results, good or bad.

  3. Gracious Professionalism

    Compete as hard as we can, and still lend a part, a laptop or an hour to the team across the pit.

  4. Open by default

    Our code, CAD and lessons belong to the next team too. We share what worked and, more usefully, what didn't.

FRC is a varsity sport
for the mind.

Every January, FIRST reveals a brand-new game to thousands of high-school teams worldwide on the same day. Then the clock starts.

8weeks

From game reveal to first competition, roughly. Design, build, wire and code a robot in that window.

3v3

Robots play in alliances of three. Your partner in one match is your opponent in the next.

vs

2:30

per match. A short autonomous period where the code drives alone, then the drivers take over.

~125lb

Seven domains.
One robot.

Students run the team. Every member picks a main domain plus extras, and every domain owns a piece of the robot or the organisation.

Domain 1 / 7

Programming

Quack the code.

Robot code in Java, autonomous routines, vision, sensors and control loops. You make the robot do exactly what it is told.

  • Java & WPILib
  • Autonomous
  • Vision
  • PID tuning

Domain 2 / 7

Mechanical

Built different. Built purple.

Fabrication, prototyping and assembly: drivetrain, intakes, arms, elevators and climbers. If it moves, it went through your hands.

  • Fabrication
  • Prototyping
  • Drivetrain
  • Mechanisms

Domain 3 / 7

Electrical

Power to the ranks.

Wiring, power distribution, CAN bus, batteries, the roboRIO and the radio. A clean electrical board wins matches nobody notices.

  • Wiring
  • CAN & power
  • Batteries
  • roboRIO

Domain 4 / 7

CAD

Measure twice. Model once.

The whole robot is designed in 3D before metal is cut: assemblies, drawings, 3D printing and renders for the portfolio.

  • 3D modelling
  • Assemblies
  • Drawings
  • 3D printing

Domain 5 / 7

Strategy & Scouting

Know the field first.

Game analysis, scouting data, match strategy and alliance selection. You decide what the robot should be good at, and who we pick.

  • Game analysis
  • Scouting data
  • Match strategy
  • Drive team

Domain 6 / 7

Business & Outreach

Leading from the front.

Sponsors, budgets, community outreach, the Impact Award and team documentation. The domain that keeps the team funded and known.

  • Sponsorship
  • Outreach
  • Impact Award
  • Budget

Domain 7 / 7

Media

Lights. Camera. Robot.

Photos, video, social media, graphics and branding. You are the reason anyone outside the Maker Lab knows what happened inside it.

  • Photo & video
  • Social
  • Design
  • Branding

Partner with us.

A first season is the most expensive one. Partners decide how far a new team gets, and put their name in front of the engineers of tomorrow.

Where your support goes

  • RobotMotors, electronics, aluminium, tools and spares to build a competitive machine.
  • CompetitionEvent registration for the Istanbul Regional, 26–28 March 2027.
  • TravelGetting the team and the robot from Mumbai to Istanbul.
  • OutreachDemos, workshops and open-source material for other schools.

What partners get

  • Your logo on the robot, team jerseys and pit banner
  • Recognition on this website and our social channels
  • A feature in our engineering portfolio and award submissions
  • A robot demonstration at your office
  • Season updates straight from the students

Financial and in-kind support are both welcome: machining, 3D printing, electronics, software licences, transport or an engineer's time.

How would you like to support us?

Join
the team.

No robotics experience needed. If you are a CAJCS student who likes building, coding, designing, filming or organising, there is a domain for you.

Who can join?

CAJCS students. Our mentors confirm the grades for each intake; tell us yours in the form and we will tell you if this intake fits.

How much time does it take?

Regular sessions in the Maker Lab through the year, with more during build season (January to March). Exact times are shared with members.

Do I need to know how to code or build?

No. Our training programme starts from the basics, and you learn by doing, with mentors beside you.

For parents: is it safe?

Work happens in the school Maker Lab under mentor supervision, with safety glasses and training before any power tool. We never publish a student's name or photo without your written consent.
